Equity Returns & Factor Dashboard

Compare long-run equity index returns against AQR's published long/short factor premia. Toggle series on and off to explore dispersion across markets and correlation among factors.

Growth of 100 compounding each series' own monthly return, rebased to 100 at the start of the selected date range (log scale). By default, Value, BAB, and QMJ show a fully collateralized total return (their long/short spread plus the T-bill rate); switch on excess returns to see the pure long/short premium instead.

Rolling window: 12 months

Rolling 12-month return

Trailing 12-month compounded return, recalculated each month, over the selected date range. Wide dispersion between lines in the same month shows how differently these exposures behaved over the same period.

Rolling 12-month volatility

Annualized standard deviation of monthly returns over the trailing 12 months, recalculated each month. Try shortening the window above: returns swing wildly while volatility stays comparatively stable — a reminder that risk is far more predictable than return.

Correlation window: Full range

Pairwise correlation of monthly returns

Computed over each pair's overlapping monthly history within the selected window. Values near +1 move together; values near 0 or negative are diversifying. Use the legend above to change which series are included.

Distribution of monthly returns

Histogram of monthly returns for each selected series over the selected date range, with a dashed normal-distribution curve of the same mean and standard deviation overlaid. Where the bars depart from the dashed curve — fatter tails, skew — real returns are departing from the normal-distribution assumption used in most risk models.
