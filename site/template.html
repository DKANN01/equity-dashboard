Equity Returns & Factor Dashboard

Compare long-run equity index returns against AQR's published long/short factor premia. Toggle series on and off to explore dispersion across markets and correlation among factors.

Growth of 100 compounding each series' own monthly return, rebased to 100 at the start of the selected date range (log scale). Factor series (Value, BAB, QMJ, Momentum) compound a long/short or long-only excess return and are not directly investable in this form — they illustrate the premium's behavior over time, not a tradable product.

Trailing 12-month compounded return, recalculated each month, over the selected date range. Wide dispersion between lines in the same month shows how differently these exposures behaved over the same period.

Pairwise correlation of monthly returns

Computed over each pair's overlapping monthly history (see tiles above for each series' date range). Values near +1 move together; values near 0 or negative are diversifying. Use the legend above to change which series are included.
